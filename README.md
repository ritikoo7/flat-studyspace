# 📚 FLAT Studyspace

### 🌱 Your Personal Space to Learn, Organize, and Revise

FLAT Studyspace is a web-based study dashboard designed to help students prepare for **Formal Languages and Automata Theory (FLAT)**. It brings syllabus tracking, task management, study notes, revision tools, and focus sessions together in one place.

Whether you're preparing for university examinations, revising difficult concepts, or organizing your daily study routine, FLAT Studyspace helps you make your learning more structured and manageable.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Study Dashboard:** Track your overall study progress.
- 📖 **Syllabus Tracker:** Mark topics as completed and monitor progress.
- ✅ **Task Manager:** Organize study tasks and deadlines.
- 📅 **Study Calendar:** Plan study sessions and important dates.
- ⏱️ **Focus Timer:** Stay focused during study sessions.
- 🧠 **Quick Revision:** Revise concepts using flashcards and revision tools.
- 📝 **Study Notes:** Write and manage your personal notes.
- 🌙 **Dark Mode:** Switch between light and dark themes.
- 💾 **Backup & Restore:** Export and import your saved study data.
- ▶️ **Learning Resources:** Search YouTube for topic explanations.

## 💡 Recommended Study Workflow

Students can use the following routine with FLAT Studyspace:

1. **Check your syllabus:** Identify the topics you need to study.
2. **Set daily goals:** Add a few achievable tasks.
3. **Learn a topic:** Study from your class notes, textbooks, or learning resources.
4. **Write notes:** Summarize the concept in your own words.
5. **Test yourself:** Use revision tools and flashcards where available.
6. **Track completion:** Mark the topic as completed when you are comfortable with it.
7. **Revise regularly:** Revisit difficult topics before your examinations.

> 💡 **Study tip:** Understanding one topic properly is more valuable than marking ten topics complete without understanding them.
